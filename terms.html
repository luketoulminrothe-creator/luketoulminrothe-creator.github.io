Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 25 August 2026

These terms cover taking classes at DancePointe and using this website. The detailed payment terms you agree to when you join are in the standing order mandate; where the two differ, the mandate applies.

Who we are

DancePointe is a dance school at 5 Swinegate, Grantham, Lincolnshire NG31 6RJ, teaching students from 2.5 years to adult. These terms apply to anyone taking classes with us and to anyone using this website. You can reach us on 07515 571655 or at info@dancepointegrantham.com.

Joining and class placement

Every student is placed in a class and level by the School Principal, not by age alone. Please speak to us before assuming which class to attend, so that your dancer is put where they will benefit most.

A place is confirmed once you have completed our enrolment paperwork and the first payment has been arranged. We may move a student between levels during the year where that is in their interest, and we will always discuss it with you first.

Fees

Fees are charged either by monthly standing order across twelve months, or by term. Standing order works out considerably cheaper: £5.84 a class against roughly £9.50 a class by term, once the administrative fee is included.

The full tables are on our fees page. Any classes taken beyond the eleventh in a week are free.

The school year runs to thirty-nine taught weeks across six terms. The monthly standing order spreads those thirty-nine weeks evenly across twelve months, which is why payments continue through the holidays.

Paying

  • Standing order is due on the 1st of each month and runs continuously over twelve months.
  • Termly fees paid by cash or cheque are due in full in the first week of each term. Cheques should be made payable to DancePointe.
  • A further discount is available on a yearly payment. Please ask at reception.

The full payment terms are set out in the standing order mandate you sign when you join. Where anything here differs from that mandate, the mandate applies.

No payments are taken through this website.

Missed classes

Classes must be paid for even when absent. We hold the place and the teacher whether or not a student attends, so we cannot credit or refund individual missed classes.

If a student is going to be away for an extended period through illness or injury, please talk to us. We would rather find something workable than lose a dancer.

Leaving the school

If your dancer is stopping, please tell the School Principal directly rather than simply not attending, and cancel any standing order with your bank once we have confirmed the final payment date. The notice we require is set out in your enrolment paperwork.

If we have to cancel a class

Occasionally a class cannot run — teacher illness, severe weather, or a problem with the building. We will let you know as early as we can, usually through Facebook and by direct message. Where practical we will reschedule the class or arrange cover.

Uniform, shoes, examinations and costumes

Uniform, dance shoes, examination entry fees and show costumes are not included in your tuition fees and are charged separately. We will always tell you the cost in advance rather than presenting you with a bill afterwards.

Nothing needs buying before a first class. Come in something you can move in, and ask at reception before spending anything — fit matters more than brand.

Examinations, the competitive team and the show

  • Examinations. Students in graded classes are entered when the School Principal judges them ready, not automatically at the end of a year. Entry fees are set by the examining body and charged separately.
  • The competitive team. Places are by invitation from the School Principal, based on demonstrated commitment and proficiency. Festival and competition entry fees, travel and costumes are additional.
  • The annual show. Our show involves the whole school. Taking part is optional, but every student who wants to be in it is in it. Costume and ticket arrangements are confirmed each year.

In the studio

Please arrive on time and collect younger students promptly. Parents and carers are responsible for their children before and after class, including in the entrance and on Swinegate itself.

We expect students to treat teachers, each other and the studios with respect. We reserve the right to ask a student to leave a class, or the school, where behaviour puts others at risk or repeatedly disrupts teaching. We would always discuss this with you first.

Health, injury and safety

Dance is physical, and acrobatic work carries risk even when taught in careful progression. Please tell us in writing about any medical condition, injury or additional need that affects your dancer, and keep us updated. Our teachers will adapt where they can.

Students take part at their own risk in respect of ordinary injury that is not caused by our negligence. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.

Photography and social media

We photograph and film classes, examinations, festivals and the annual show, and we use some of those images on this website and on our Facebook and Instagram pages. Consent is recorded on your enrolment paperwork and you can withdraw it at any time by telling the School Principal.

Please do not photograph or film other people's children in the studio or at the theatre without their parents' agreement, and do not post such images publicly.

Your belongings

Please do not leave valuables in the changing areas. We cannot accept responsibility for personal property lost or damaged on the premises.

Changes to the timetable or fees

The timetable is reviewed each year and can change during the year where numbers or staffing require it. Fees are reviewed annually. We will give you as much notice as we reasonably can of any change that affects you.
