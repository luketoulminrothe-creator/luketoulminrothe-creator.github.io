Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information DancePointe collects through this website, why we collect it, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. It is written to describe what the site actually does rather than to cover every eventuality.

Who we are

DancePointe is a dance school at 5 Swinegate, Grantham, Lincolnshire NG31 6RJ. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website and in the course of teaching. You can reach us on 07515 571655 or at info@dancepointegrantham.com.

What this website collects

Only what you type into the enquiry form. There is nothing else: no account to create, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no payments taken through the site.

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • The dancer's age, if you tell us
  • The style of class you are asking about
  • Anything you write in the message box

Fees are arranged directly with the school by standing order, bank transfer, cash or cheque. No card or bank details are ever entered on this website.

Enquiries about children

Most enquiries are made by a parent or guardian about a child, and the form asks for the dancer's age. The form is intended to be completed by an adult. Please do not ask a child to fill it in on your behalf.

We ask for an age only so that the School Principal can suggest a suitable class and level. We do not ask for a child's name, address, school, photograph or any health information through this website. Information of that kind is collected separately when a student actually joins, and is covered by the enrolment paperwork rather than this policy.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry, to recommend a class and level, and to arrange a first visit to the studio.

Our lawful basis is that the processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us. We do not send marketing to enquirers who do not join, and we never sell or rent your details.

Who else is involved

Two third parties are involved in running this website. Both are named here because your data reaches them, not ours alone.

  • Web3Forms receives the enquiry form and forwards it to our inbox. They act as a processor for us. Their servers are outside the UK, so submitting the form involves an international transfer of the details you have entered.
  • Google Maps is embedded on our home and contact pages to show where the studio is. When that map loads, Google receives your IP address and basic device information, and may set its own cookies. This happens because the map is displayed, not because you interacted with it.
  • Our website host keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and diagnostics.

We may also share information where the law requires it, or where we have a safeguarding duty to do so.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a student joining are deleted within twelve months. If you do join, your details move into our student records, which we keep while you are with the school and for a reasonable period afterwards, and for longer where accounting or safeguarding rules require it. You can ask us to delete an enquiry sooner at any time.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. When you accept or decline the cookie notice, that choice alone is saved in your own browser's local storage so we do not ask you again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

The embedded Google map may set cookies of its own. See our cookie policy for more.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have anything inaccurate corrected
  • ask us to delete it, subject to any legal obligation to keep records
  • object to or restrict how we use it
  • withdraw consent where we relied on it

Write to info@dancepointegrantham.com or ring 07515 571655 and we will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, the UK supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change we will update this page and the date at the top. This version was published on 25 August 2026.
