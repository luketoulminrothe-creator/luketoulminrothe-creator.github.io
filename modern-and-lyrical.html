What we cover

Every modern and lyrical class we run

Grades 1 & 2 Modern

Thursday, 4.45pm. Where graded modern starts: travelling steps, kicks, turns, and the beginnings of a genuine performance quality.

Grades 3 & 4 Modern

Thursday, 5.30pm. The syllabus gets properly athletic, and students start to be trusted with their own interpretation of a phrase.

Grades 5 & 6 Modern

Friday, 5.00pm. Senior graded modern, and the last stop before the advanced classes and the examination stream.

Advanced Modern 1 and 2

Advanced 1 on Saturday at 3.45pm, Advanced Modern 2 on Wednesday at 4.00pm for ninety minutes. The top of the modern syllabus.

Inter Modern Exam Class

Tuesday, 4.00pm, ninety minutes. A dedicated class for students preparing an Intermediate examination, rather than exam prep squeezed into a normal lesson.

Lyrical: Inter, Senior, Advanced

Inter and Senior Lyrical on Monday at 4.00pm, Advanced Lyrical at 4.45pm. Contemporary movement, storytelling, and the style that tends to win festivals.

Joining us

How you start, whatever the style

1

Get in touch

Call 07515 571655 or send the form. Tell us the age, and which styles they fancy trying. If you have no idea, say that instead. Most people do.

2

Every student is placed by the School Principal, not by age alone. Rosie will talk it through and recommend the class and level that will benefit your dancer most.

3

Turn up at 5 Swinegate for the class we have agreed. Something they can move in is plenty to start with. Uniform and shoes can wait until you know it has stuck.

4

Add styles, work towards graded exams, be invited into the competitive team, dance in the annual show. Or keep the one class you love. Both are fine here.

Pricing

What modern and lyrical cost here

One class a week is £25 a month on standing order, taken over twelve months. Two classes are £38, three are £57, and every class after that adds £19, up to £209 for eleven, after which anything extra is free. Students in the modern stream usually take two or three classes a week, which lands most families at £38 to £57 a month.

What affects the price

  • • How many classes a week across every style, and modern students often take ballet alongside
  • • Whether you add the Inter Modern Exam Class in the examination year
  • • Standing order versus termly payment: the gap over a year is roughly £72 on a single class and £288 on two
  • • Examination entry fees, charged separately by the examining body
  • • Festival and competition entry fees, if your dancer is invited into the competitive team

Every class is counted the same way regardless of length, so the ninety-minute Advanced Modern 2 and the 45-minute Grade 1 both count as one class on the fee table. That is deliberate, and it favours the students doing the most work.

We also handle

Ballet

Classical ballet from Intro Ballet at 2.5, through the graded syllabus, to Pre-Vocational 1 and Pre-Vocational Advanced 2. Taught by a Central School of Ballet graduate and a former Head of Dance at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School.
