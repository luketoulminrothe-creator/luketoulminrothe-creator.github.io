Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026

This page explains what cookies and similar technologies we use on this website, what they do, and how you can control them.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember things between visits (e.g., that you've already dismissed a banner) and help us understand how people use the site.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary (always on):

  • dancepointe_cookie_consent remembers whether you accepted or declined non-essential cookies. Stored locally for 12 months.

Analytics (only if you accept):

  • Anonymous page-view tracking via our analytics provider. Records page URL, referrer, device type, approximate location by IP. Does not personally identify you. Cookies expire after 14 months.

Embedded forms and widgets:

  • The quote form on our site is hosted by a third party. When you load the form, that provider may set its own cookies to make the form work. We do not control those cookies, so see the provider's policy for details.

How to control cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner that appears the first time you visit. Your choice is remembered for 12 months. To change it, clear the dancepointe_cookie_consent key in your browser's site data, then reload, and the banner will reappear.

You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser settings. The site will still work, but the banner will show every visit.

Do Not Track

If your browser sends a 'Do Not Track' signal, we treat that as a decline of non-essential cookies.

Changes

If we add new cookies or change how existing ones work, we'll update this page and ask for fresh consent through the banner.
