The school

A dance school
for everyone

DancePointe is Rosie Johnson's school. Rosie trained in the East Midlands in Classical Ballet, Modern Theatre, Jazz, Tap and Acrobatic arts, performed, took her teaching qualifications, and then set up her own school in Grantham. She still teaches, coaches and choreographs here every week.

We teach from 2.5 years to adult, across a range of levels, from the complete beginner to the pre-vocational and pre-professional student. Nobody is streamed by ambition. A child who wants one tap class on a Saturday morning gets the same teaching as a student working towards vocational school.

Alongside her school, Rosie has worked with Lincolnshire County Council delivering children's speech therapy in local education authorities, helped develop a dance curriculum for children with additional needs that is now used in schools as part of PE, and choreographed Rock Challenge, pantomimes, showcases and GCSE examinations for schools across the area. She takes secondary school students for work experience.

Students can take part in technique classes, dance competitions and festivals, as well as our annual show at the Guildhall Arts Centre. We offer the highest quality of training, encouraging every student to discover the joy of dance and to reach their full potential.

The faculty

Six teachers, and between them
a great deal of stage

DancePointe has an exceptional faculty who are committed to providing the very highest standard of dance tuition. This is who your child will actually be taught by.

Rosie Johnson

School Principal & Studio Owner

Trained in the East Midlands in Classical Ballet, Modern Theatre, Jazz, Tap and Acrobatic arts. Performed, qualified as a teacher, then founded DancePointe. Helped develop a dance curriculum for children with additional needs now used in schools as part of PE, and has choreographed Rock Challenge, pantomimes and GCSE examinations for schools across the area. In the festival movement since she was a child.

Gail Gordon

Ballet & Private Coaching

A performance career as a dancer and solo acrobatic act, then professional choreographer in London’s West End, and Head of Dance and Movement at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School for over thirty years. Now Dance Director for Chantry Dance Company and Associate Director of Chantry School of Contemporary & Balletic Arts.

Elizabeth Haslam

Ballet, Tap, Jazz

Graduated from Central School of Ballet and performed with Northern Ballet Theatre, then corps de ballet in Aida with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra and soloist in Vis av Magi in Norway. Former head of ballet at the Midlands School of Dance and Drama, and original ballet mistress to Ballet Theatre UK, where she spent nine years.

Beth Warrick

Street, Commercial

Dancer and singer on UK and European touring productions including Vampires Rock, Anything for Love, Rule the World and Masters of the Scene. Lead vocalist with bands and shows around the world, model for Toni & Guy, L’Oréal and Kérastase hair shows, and in pantomime almost every year for twelve years. Also a choreographer, and runs her own competitive cheerleading programme.

Anita Harvey-Warne

Contemporary

Contemporary dancer, choreographer, and co-founder and co-artistic director of RESET Dance Company in the Midlands. Deeply committed to inclusion, diversity and acceptance in her work. Has collaborated with Parkour Circle, Tom Dale and Spiltmilk Dance, where she was an Early Career Performer Trainee on the Autumn 2023 tour of Desert Island Flicks.

Elaine Bishop

Singing / Musical Theatre

Experienced singing teacher, mezzo-soprano and award-winning musical director, based in Grantham. Built a reputation at the International Gilbert and Sullivan Festival and performed with professional opera companies. Credits include Cantata Eliensis, HMS Pinafore for Raymond Gubbay Productions, Trial by Jury and Utopia. Chairperson of Grantham Operatic Society.

Classes

Seven styles under one roof

Ballet

Classical ballet from Intro Ballet at 2.5, through the graded syllabus, to Pre-Vocational 1 and Pre-Vocational Advanced 2. Taught by a Central School of Ballet graduate and a former Head of Dance at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School.

Tap

Primary Tap, Grades 1 to 6 and Advanced Tap. Rhythm, clarity and sound, taught properly from the first shuffle. The style most of our students keep for life.

Modern & Lyrical

Graded Modern from Grade 1 to Advanced 2, an Inter Modern exam class, and Lyrical at Inter, Senior and Advanced level. Contemporary work sits here too.

Acro

Junior, Inter and Senior Acro, then Advanced Acro 1 and 2. Strength, flexibility and controlled tricks, built up in the right order rather than rushed.

Street & Commercial

Junior, Inter and Senior Street with Beth Warrick, who has danced and sung on UK and European tours, twelve years of pantomime, and hair shows for Toni & Guy and L’Oréal.

Musical Theatre & Singing

Stage & Song, Junior Musical Theatre and Senior Musical. Singing taught by an award-winning musical director and the chairperson of Grantham Operatic Society.

Track record

From Swinegate to the
Dance World Cup

2023

Dance World Cup finals

A DancePointe student trained here by Rosie represented Team England at the Dance World Cup finals in Portugal, placing 4th in ballet repertoire, 7th in modern and 8th in lyrical against a field of roughly 7,500 dancers. DancePointe families raised £400 towards the trip.

Every year

On the Guildhall stage

The whole school performs an annual show at the Guildhall Arts Centre in Grantham. Dance Dynamics, Dance Spectacular, In The Spotlight, A Dance Celebration and United in Dance have all played there. Every student who wants to be in it is in it.

All year

On the festival circuit

Our competitive team dances the festival circuit through the year, most recently at the Nottingham Dance Festival. Places on the team are by invitation from the School Principal, on commitment and proficiency, not on request.

Everything on this page is something you can check. We would rather point you at the Guildhall listings and the festival results than write our own testimonials.

Questions parents ask

The things parents actually ask

Classical Ballet, Modern, Contemporary, Tap, Street, Acrobatics and Musical Theatre, with singing taught alongside the musical theatre classes. Lyrical runs at Inter, Senior and Advanced level. Most students start with one or two styles and add more as they go.

We teach children from 2.5 years old, and we teach adults. Intro Ballet & Tap on a Saturday morning is where the youngest dancers start. Please ring to check what is currently running for adults, as the adult timetable moves around more than the children's does.

We offer classes for all levels. Please consult with the School Principal, who will advise you which level class to attend and ensure that you are placed in a level which will benefit you the most. Nobody is put in a class on age alone.

To be invited to join the school's competitive team, students are required to demonstrate a high level of commitment to dance and a specific level of proficiency. The School Principal will invite students to take part who have the appropriate level. We compete on the festival circuit through the year, most recently at the Nottingham Dance Festival.

Yes. Each year we present an annual show which involves the entire school, staged at the Guildhall Arts Centre in Grantham. Past shows include Dance Dynamics, Dance Spectacular, In The Spotlight, A Dance Celebration and United in Dance. Every student who wants to be in it is in it.
