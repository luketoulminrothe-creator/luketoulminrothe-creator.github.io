Tap

The one they never give up.

Primary Tap through Grades 1 to 6 and on to Advanced. Tap is the style students come back to at forty, and the one that teaches musicality faster than anything else we run.

Enquire now Call 07515 571655

Teaching students from Grantham, Barrowby, Great Gonerby, Belton, Harlaxton, Bottesford, Long Bennington and the villages around them.

What we cover

Every tap class we run

Intro Ballet & Tap, 2.5+

Saturday, 9.00am. The first taste of tap, taught alongside ballet in one 45-minute class so the youngest dancers get both without a two-hour morning.

Primary Tap

Saturday, 9.45am. Shuffles, taps, and the beginning of hearing your own feet. The class where rhythm stops being an accident.

Grades 1 & 2 Tap

Saturday, 12.00pm. Two grades in one room, working the syllabus that carries students through their first tap examinations.

Grades 3 & 4 Tap

Saturday, 1.30pm. Faster, cleaner, and the point at which most students discover that tap is harder than it looks and more fun than they expected.

Grades 5 & 6 Tap

Saturday, 3.00pm. Senior graded work: genuine speed, genuine clarity, and combinations that would not be out of place in a show.

Advanced Tap

Tuesday, 6.15pm. The top of the school. Technically demanding, and where our festival and competition tap numbers are built.

Joining us

How you start, whatever the style

1

Get in touch

Call 07515 571655 or send the form. Tell us the age, and which styles they fancy trying. If you have no idea, say that instead. Most people do.

2

Rosie places the level

Every student is placed by the School Principal, not by age alone. Rosie will talk it through and recommend the class and level that will benefit your dancer most.

3

Come and dance

Turn up at 5 Swinegate for the class we have agreed. Something they can move in is plenty to start with. Uniform and shoes can wait until you know it has stuck.

4

Go as far as you want

Add styles, work towards graded exams, be invited into the competitive team, dance in the annual show. Or keep the one class you love. Both are fine here.

Pricing

What tap costs here

One class a week is £25 a month on standing order, taken over twelve months. Two classes are £38, three are £57, and every class after that adds £19, up to £209 for eleven, after which anything extra is free. Tap is the classic second class, and the second class only adds £13 a month to a single-class fee.

What affects the price

  • • How many classes a week in total, across every style: they are counted together, not per style
  • • Standing order (£5.84 a class) versus paying by term (nearer £9.50 a class)
  • • Examination entry fees for the graded classes, charged separately when a student is entered
  • • Tap shoes, which you buy. Ask at reception before you spend; sizing matters more than brand
  • • Nothing else. There is no joining fee and no registration fee

If your dancer already does one class, adding tap is £13 a month. If they end up doing eleven classes a week, everything beyond the eleventh is free. That is not a sales line, it is written into the fee table.
